# MealHQ brand

Canadian tiffin ops SaaS — “HQ for the kitchen.” Fresh identity; not derived from prior product art.

## Palette

| Token  | Hex       | Role                    |
|--------|-----------|-------------------------|
| Ink    | `#0B1220` | Primary type / mark     |
| Mango  | `#F5A524` | Accent (meals / energy) |
| Lagoon | `#0E8F8B` | Secondary (ops / routes)|
| Mist   | `#F4F7F6` | Light surfaces          |
| White  | `#FFFFFF` | Canvas                  |

Avoid: purple gradients, cream + terracotta serif kitsch, neon glow, heavy soft shadows.

## Mark

Geometric app-icon tile: plate arc + upward route chevron that reads as **M**. Strong at 16×16 and 1024×1024.

Wordmark: bold geometric sans. “Meal” in Ink, “HQ” in Lagoon.

## Usage

- Prefer horizontal lockup in headers at ~28–36px height.
- Use mark alone for favicon, PWA, and compact nav.
- On dark surfaces use `mealhq-mark-dark.png` or invert carefully; do not tint Mango/Lagoon out of palette.
